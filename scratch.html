Interior Design - The Art of Home

How color, texture, and structure turn a house to a home

My passion for interior design began in 2022 at my first job working in a furniture showroom and design service center. The creative process of combining elelments and textures to create inviting spaces captivated me. I learned how color texure, pattern, material, and shape all combine harmoiniously to make a room feel as if it has always existed. I believe a home is the foundation of a family and strive to create spaces that are warm, comfortable, practical, and inviting to all those who need a place to call home.

My Design Process: From House to Home

Here is how I approach a new space.

  1. Draw and study the room's natural light and layout
  2. Choose a color palette - My favorites include:
    • Warm neutrals and burgandy
    • Soft creamy yellows
    • Cream and ivory tones
    • Airy baby blues and white
  3. Select furniture that is comfortable to live in and stylish
  4. Select textiles, art, and lighting for greater depth
  5. Step back, adjust, and complete each detail
